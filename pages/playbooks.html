Playbooks / Workflows from start to finish

Make the whole process easier

A playbook breaks a larger task into steps you can review, adapt, and repeat. Keep a person in charge of decisions and final work.

Playbook 01 / Reporting

Prepare a board pack with AI

Use AI to organize a pack from approved source material. Keep financial and strategic claims tied to verified documents.

  1. Gather the approved reports, agreed metrics, audience, and required format.
  2. Ask for a proposed outline and a list of missing inputs before drafting.
  3. Draft one section at a time, asking the tool to distinguish source facts from suggested narrative.
  4. Check every number, date, citation, and statement against its source.
  5. Have the accountable owner review the complete pack and approve it before distribution.

Playbook 02 / Getting started

Onboard to a new project

Turn project information you are permitted to use into a structured orientation and a useful set of questions.

  1. Collect approved project materials and identify what is current, authoritative, or still uncertain.
  2. Ask for a concise overview of goals, stakeholders, milestones, dependencies, and open questions.
  3. Compare the overview with the source documents; correct gaps and keep references beside claims.
  4. Use the verified overview to prepare a first-week plan and questions for the project lead.

Adapt every workflow to your organization's approved tools, confidentiality rules, and review requirements. Never let a generated draft bypass an existing approval process.
