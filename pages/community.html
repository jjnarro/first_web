Frequently asked questions

A few useful guardrails

Can I share a prompt that includes real work information?

Only share information that is approved for the destination and audience. Remove personal, client, confidential, and regulated details unless explicit policy allows them.

Can I publish a time-saved estimate?

Yes, when it is measured against a clear baseline. Explain the sample, review effort, and quality checks rather than presenting an unverified estimate as a fact.

Where should I ask a question?

Use your organization's established AI community or ask your manager for the current support contact. A direct contact link can be added here once one is provided.
