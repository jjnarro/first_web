What could you do with AI?

Start with the work in front of you. These ideas are prompts for experimentation, not a substitute for approved processes or professional judgment.

Browse by task

Common work, new approaches

Writing & Communication

Draft, revise, adjust tone, and adapt a message for a specific audience. Try a writing prompt.

Research & Analysis

Generate questions, compare supplied material, and organize evidence to investigate. Verify findings with primary sources.

Summarising & Synthesis

Extract themes, decisions, or open questions from material you are allowed to share. Keep source context close.

Planning & Decision Support

Map options, dependencies, risks, and next steps. Treat recommendations as inputs, not automated decisions.

Data & Spreadsheets

Explain formulas, suggest ways to structure data, or help interpret a chart. Test formulas and calculations against known cases.

Meetings & Collaboration

Prepare an agenda, clarify action items, or turn approved notes into a follow-up. Respect consent and recording rules.

Browse by role / function

Start with your team's context

Finance

Explain variance drivers, draft commentary, and explore scenarios using approved, verified data.

Human Resources

Draft inclusive communications, structure role descriptions, and organize policy material for review.

Sales

Prepare account research questions, tailor approved messaging, and summarize public customer information.

Operations

Document processes, identify handoffs, and turn recurring questions into draft procedures.

Legal

Organize questions and summarize approved documents for qualified review. Do not rely on AI for legal advice.

IT

Draft internal guidance, explain technical concepts, and structure support knowledge for review.

Use approved tools and follow your organization's rules for confidential data, regulated work, and decisions that affect people.
